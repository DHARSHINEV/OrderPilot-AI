To gather first-hand qualitative and observational evidence regarding how small business operators receive, verify, calculate, and enter conversational customer orders.

## Interview Questions

### 1. Ingestion & Channels
- How do you currently receive customer orders outside of formal web stores?
- What messaging applications or platforms are most common (WhatsApp, Instagram, Email, SMS, Phone)?
- Can you describe the most typical format of an incoming message?

### 2. Manual Workflow & Transcription
- Once a message arrives, what is the exact sequence of steps you or your staff take to enter it into your records?
- Do you use a spreadsheet, billing software, notebook, or ERP?
- How much time does entering a typical order take from arrival to confirmation?

### 3. Inventory & Pricing Checks
- How do you verify stock availability before confirming an order?
- What happens when a customer requests an item that is out of stock or low in stock?
- How often do pricing or calculation mistakes happen during manual arithmetic?

### 4. Ambiguity & Missing Information
- What information is most frequently missing from customer messages (e.g., size, color, delivery address, phone number)?
- How do you handle follow-up communication when details are ambiguous?

### 5. Trust, Autonomy & Human Approval
- What would make you trust an AI-assisted order processing system?
- Which specific decisions would you **always** want to approve yourself before anything is finalized?
- Would you ever want an AI agent to send messages directly to your customers without your explicit review?

### 6. Prototype Demonstration & Usability Feedback
- *[Demonstrate OrderPilot AI input &rarr; agent orchestration &rarr; approval queue workflow]*
- What are your immediate impressions of the agent's timeline and extracted breakdown?
- How does the Human Approval Queue fit into your existing daily routine?
- What concerns, if any, remain regarding accuracy or control?
